## Brand & Style

This design system draws deeply from Material Design 3 (Material You) with an expressive, empathetic twist tailored for an AI-powered emotional journaling sanctuary. The brand identity balances thoughtful mindfulness with ambient intelligence—acting not as a clinical tracker, but as a gentle, receptive companion that listens, reflects, and illuminates internal growth.

The target audience spans mindful individuals, reflective thinkers, and wellness enthusiasts seeking a sanctuary for daily vulnerability. The interface elicits warmth, emotional clarity, psychological safety, and optimism. 

The aesthetic philosophy centers on **Tonal Warmth & Modern Softness**:
- **Clean Bright Surfaces**: Pure light foundations juxtaposed with soft, organic pastel tints that shift dynamically based on captured moods.
- **Organic Geometry**: Friendly, rounded corners and pill-shaped touchpoints that eliminate sharp friction points.
- **Atmospheric Luminance**: Layered elevation expressed through color-shifted tonal containers and ultra-soft, diffused ambient shadows rather than harsh divider lines.

## Colors

The palette embraces the expressive nature of Material You, grounded in soothing pastel-tinted tones that evoke therapeutic harmony:

- **Primary (`#6750A4`)**: Soft twilight violet. Anchors intentional focus, reflection, and introspective depth. Its lighter container tints (`#EADDFF`) provide soothing resting grounds for interactive focus.
- **Secondary (`#E06880`)**: Gentle blooming rose. Communicates empathy, heart-centered vulnerability, and sentiment insights.
- **Tertiary (`#E5A000`)**: Sunny golden amber. Embodies warmth, optimistic energy, and moments of gratitude and personal triumph.
- **Accent Mint (`#38A3A5`)**: An auxiliary emotion accent evoking calm, renewal, recovery, and inner balance.
- **Surface & Backgrounds**: Pure pristine surfaces (`#FEFBFF`) transition into progressively rich tonal container tiers (`#F3EDF7`, `#ECE6F0`, `#E6E0E9`) to structure hierarchy organically without visual clutter.

## Typography

The type system relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles, selected for its contemporary geometry, rounded letter terminals, open apertures, and warm approachability. It matches the expressive nature of Material Design 3 while remaining legible for both long-form reflective journaling and micro-metric sentiment badges.

- **Display & Headlines**: Generous, softly curved titles set the tone for reflective inquiries (e.g., "How is your heart feeling today?").
- **Body Hierarchy**: Tuned with open line-heights (1.6x–1.65x) to optimize legibility during extended personal writing and reading AI insights.
- **Labels**: High-weight, slightly tracked tokens explicitly engineered for emotional tags, pills, and state chips.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a **fluid, breathing responsive grid** aligned with Material 3 responsive guidelines:

- **Breakpoints**: Mobile (`0–599px`, 4 columns, `margin-sm`), Tablet (`600–1023px`, 8 columns, `margin`), Desktop (`1024px+`, 12 columns, max-width `1280px` centered container).
- **Rhythm & Padding**: An 8pt base grid with a 4pt sub-grid dictates spacing. Ample white space is treated as an active design element, preventing emotional overwhelm and maintaining cognitive clarity.
- **Journal Canvas**: Diary reading and writing panels default to a comfortable single-column reading column (`680px` max width) on desktop to foster intimacy and focus.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **M3 tonal color layering paired with ambient, pastel-tinted light diffusion**:

- **Tonal Elevation (Surfaces 0–5)**: Rather than stark gray drop shadows, elevation is initially defined through surface container fills. Higher elevation tiers shift into soft, primary-tinted pastel whites.
- **Ambient Shadows**: Shadow colors are never pure black or stark gray. They incorporate 4% to 8% alpha blends of the primary indigo-violet (`rgba(103, 80, 164, 0.08)`), softened with extended blur radii (up to `24px`) to create an ethereal, floating feel.
- **Micro-interactions**: Hovering or dragging journal cards triggers gentle vertical translation (-2px) accompanied by a luminous spread of the ambient pastel shadow.
- **Scrims & Overlays**: Modals and emotional reflection drawers utilize frosted surface blurring (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) overlaid with a 20% alpha primary tint, ensuring the user's ongoing context remains gently visible.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy establishes an organic, friendly rhythm across UI tiers:

- **Full Pills (`9999px`)**: Reserved for primary interactive controls, emotional sentiment chips, mood filter pills, and search bars.
- **Rounded Surfaces (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem / 24px)**: Standard for primary journaling cards, modal canvases, and AI summary modules, removing sharp edges to convey comfort.
- **Interactive Inputs (`rounded-lg` / 1rem / 16px)**: Applied to text input fields, mood sliders, and secondary grouped list containers.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Badges
- **Filled Primary Button**: Height `48px`, full pill shape (`rounded-full`), solid `#6750A4` fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Supports subtle ambient violet drop shadow on hover.
- **Tonal Secondary Button**: Full pill shape, filled with soft primary-container tint (`#EADDFF`), text colored `#21005D`. Provides a gentler alternative for secondary reflection prompts.
- **Emotion Filter Badges / Chips**: Height `32px`, pill-shaped, bordered with subtle 1px tonal lines (`#CAC4D0`) when unselected. When active, shifts to full pastel mood fill (e.g., gentle rose `#FFD8E4` for Gratitude, mint `#C4EED0` for Serenity) with a leading 8px glowing mood dot.

### Diary Cards & AI Summary Cards
- **Journal Entry Card**: Enclosed in `rounded-xl` (24px) with background `#FFFFFF`, surrounded by a gentle ambient violet shadow (`0 4px 20px -2px rgba(103, 80, 164, 0.06)`). Padding set to `space-lg` (24px).
- **AI Emotion Insight Card**: Elevated with a subtle gradient wash from soft violet (`#F3EDF7`) to pastel rose (`#FCEEF0`), featuring a pill badge header denoting key emotion keywords (e.g., "Reflective", "Hopeful").

### Text Inputs & Diary Editor
- **Canvas Text Area**: Borderless in reading/writing view; uses a soft tonal container (`#F3EDF7`) in bounded mode with `16px` inner radius. The active state employs a 2px inner focus stroke in primary violet (`#6750A4`) without harsh exterior outlines.
- **Micro-Prompts**: In-line AI reflection prompts appear inside dynamic floating chips with a gentle mint or amber pastel highlight.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 20px circular and rounded-sm indicators with smooth SVG state animations; selected states transition from neutral border into solid `#6750A4` with a clean white icon check.
- **Mood Rating Track**: Fluid continuous slider with an oversized 24px pill thumb, tracking smoothly along a track filled with dynamic pastel emotion gradients (violet → mint → warm amber).
